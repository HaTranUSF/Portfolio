Player scouting & decision support

Soccer Analytics & Scouting

Player stats are easy to find, but they are not always easy to compare. A midfielder and a striker are judged differently, and raw totals can be misleading when players have different minutes or play in different leagues. I built this project to make those comparisons more useful, from cleaning and modeling the data to creating per-90 and position-aware metrics and a scouting app for comparing 4,500+ players.

Workforce demand & market visibility

Data Jobs Market

I started this because I wanted a clearer picture of the data job market than I could get from scrolling through individual postings. I collected and cleaned 5,500+ USAJOBS postings across 12 role categories, then built a Power BI dashboard to compare demand, salary, location, and work arrangement. I also moved the project from Snowflake to PostgreSQL after realizing the simpler setup made more sense for what I was actually building.

Targeting efficiency & marketing cost

Coupon Acceptance

The question here was not just whether I could predict coupon acceptance. I wanted to know whether the model could make targeting more useful for the business. I worked with 12,700+ records, built features around customer behavior and context, and evaluated the model with the cost of different mistakes in mind. The final approach was estimated to cut distribution costs by 3.1% compared with sending coupons without targeting.
